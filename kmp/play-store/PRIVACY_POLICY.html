Privacy Policy: Udukkai Textiles and Udukkai Factory

Who we are

Udukkai Textiles and Udukkai Factory are billing apps developed, owned and operated by Elvan Navil. Effective date: November 2026.

Data on your phone

Your business details, customers, items, bills, receipts, logo and signature are stored in the app's database on your phone. Bill PDFs and backups are saved in the Documents folder of your phone.

Google account and sync

When you sign in with an approved Google account, your email address, name and account picture are used to sign you in and are shown in the app. Your data is synced to Google's Firebase Realtime Database so that it is available on your other devices. The data is encrypted in transit.

Usage statistics

The app uses Firebase Analytics to understand how it is used (device details and app events). The content of your bills is not sent for this.

What we do not do

The app shows no advertisements, does not sell your data and does not share it with anyone except Google (Firebase), which processes it for us.

Your control

You can turn on the app lock, sign out, delete a business or data, and save or restore backups.

Deleting your data

To remove your data, delete the business or the records in the app; the synced copy is deleted too. To ask us to delete your account data, email elvanpartha@gmail.com from the account you signed in with.

Children

This application is for business billing and is not directed at children under the age of 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, data requests or concerns: elvanpartha@gmail.com
